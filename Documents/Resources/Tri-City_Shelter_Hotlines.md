# Tri-City & Hayward Shelter & Emergency Directory

Keep these numbers accessible on your mobile phone during street outreach.

---

## Alameda County 2-1-1 Coordinated Entry

* **Phone:** Dial `2-1-1` or text `898211`
* **Website:** [edenir.org](https://edenir.org)
* **Hours:** 24 hours / 7 days a week
* **Scope:** Universal intake for emergency shelter, domestic violence safe houses, warming centers, and housing navigators.
