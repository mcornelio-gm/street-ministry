# Quarterly Street Ministry Orientation & De-escalation Workshop

Are you feeling called to join our street teams or support behind-the-scenes? We are holding our quarterly **Volunteer Orientation and Street Safety Workshop**.

### Workshop Details:
* **Date:** Saturday, November 21, 2026
* **Time:** 10:00 AM – 1:30 PM (Lunch provided)
* **Location:** Resonate Community Room (Fremont Campus)
* **Topics Covered:**
  * Trauma-informed engagement and street etiquette
  * Active listening when encountering substance use or mental health distress
  * Practical food safety, hot beverage handling, and kit assembly
  * Walking mock routes and team coordination
