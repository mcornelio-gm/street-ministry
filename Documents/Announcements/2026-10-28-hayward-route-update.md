- The Newark Jacks serve the area around the Jack in the Box near Dakota Road, generally walking through the surrounding area. **The route has experienced several team-member changes,** but the leader expressed gratitude for the unique strengths each volunteer brings.

- The team shared a recent example of practical support: donations helped Sergio, a street friend stranded in Casper, Wyoming, purchase a Greyhound ticket home. He began the 46-hour trip and was expected to return to the Hayward area later that week.

- Another long-term example involved a street friend becoming an in-home caregiver for a volunteer’s mother-in-law. The arrangement has continued successfully for several years, showing how trust and relationships can create meaningful opportunities for stability and employment.

- The stories emphasized that unhoused people are individuals with abilities, relationships, and potential, despite difficult circumstances.
