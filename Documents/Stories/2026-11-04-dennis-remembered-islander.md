**We received heavy news on the Islander route last night**. During a wellness check on his room, it was discovered that our longtime friend Dennis, whom we first met back in 2017, had quietly passed away--his body finally surrendering to the cancer that took him from this world.

To know Dennis was to catch a glimpse of a rich, vibrant life. Long before he called the Islander Motel home, he spent years traveling the open waters as a cruise ship entertainer, filling rooms with music from behind the piano keys. In later years, he passed on that love of music to his piano students. At the Islander, he became known as the faithful keeper of the motel cats--a gentle fixture of kindness in a place that often needs it most.

What many of us will cherish most, though, was the quiet fellowship we shared. Dennis had a genuine hunger for the sacred. Just one week after we gave him a Bible, he told us he had already finished reading through the letters to the Corinthians. Whenever we asked him to pray over the food we brought, he would do so without hesitation--and as he turned to leave with his meal, he never failed to say, "I love you guys."

Dennis lived a full song, hit some difficult notes along the way, but never lost his gentleness. Rest in peace, Dennis--we pray the Lord surrounds you with His grace.
