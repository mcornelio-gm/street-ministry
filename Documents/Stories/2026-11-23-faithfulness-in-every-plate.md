On Tuesday nights in Hayward, the residents of the Islander Motel know they can count on a familiar, welcoming sight. For years, Anne and Henry have been the steady heartbeat of the route--showing up week after week not just to deliver supplies, but to build genuine relationships with the people who call the motel home.

As co-leaders of the Islander Motel route, Anne and Henry bring a quiet, enduring consistency to the ministry. Street outreach requires patience, grace, and a willingness to show up regardless of the weather or the week's challenges. Over the years, their faithfulness has turned brief doorway interactions into deep-rooted trust. For Anne and Henry, serving on Tuesday nights is never just a task to check off; it is an opportunity to reflect the love of Christ and share the hope of the gospel with neighbors who are often overlooked.

Yet for the Anne and Henry, Tuesday nights are only the beginning. Moved by a deep compassion for the community they serve, they took it upon themselves to go the extra mile. On their own initiative, they return to the Islander Motel every other Friday to serve a full, hot-plate meal.

Anne pours her heart into the kitchen, preparing a generous variety of cuisines--including beloved, comforting Filipino dishes--that bring a taste of home and dignity to everyone on the route. Together, Anne and Henry set up, serve, and sit down to fellowship, letting every warm meal open the door for prayer, encouragement, and conversations about God's love.

This week, they are preparing a special Thanksgiving dinner for the folks at the Islander.

Through their years of steady leadership, their Friday night hospitality, and their unwavering devotion to sharing the gospel, Anne and Henry remind us what street ministry is truly about: showing up, loving faithfully, and serving others with the grace of Christ.
