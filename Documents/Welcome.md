# Loving Our Unhoused Neighbors Across the East Bay

Welcome to the official portal for **Resonate Church Street Ministry**. We are a dedicated community of volunteers, pastors, and neighbors answering the call to step outside church walls and step onto the streets, creeks, sidewalks, and motel corridors of **Fremont, Newark, Union City, and Hayward**.

> *"For I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed me."*  
> — **Matthew 25:35**

---

## Our Heart & Mission

Every week, hundreds of men, women, and families in our local East Bay cities sleep without a permanent roof over their heads—in tents beneath overpasses, tucked inside creek brush, resting in aging vehicles, or surviving day-to-day in budget transit motels.

Our mission is simple and unwavering:
1. **Be Present:** Consistency builds trust. We show up week in and week out with warm coffee, hearty meals, and open hearts.
2. **Meet Immediate Needs:** Distributing hygiene packs, season-appropriate clothing, tarps, blankets, sleeping bags, and non-perishable pantry packs.
3. **Build Authentic Relationships:** Recognizing every individual's inherent dignity, listening to their stories, and standing with them without judgment.
4. **Bridge to Resources:** Partnering with local shelters, mobile clinics, recovery ministries, and housing navigators in Alameda County.
