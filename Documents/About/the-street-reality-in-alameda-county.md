---
title: The Street Reality in Alameda County
date: 2026-11-18
author: Street Ministry Leadership
summary: Understanding the diverse circumstances of our unhoused neighbors across creek encampments, vehicles, motels, and senior or youth displacement.
---

Homelessness in the Tri-City and Hayward region is multifaceted:

- **Creek Encampments:** Tucked into the riparian corridors of Alameda Creek (Fremont) and San Lorenzo Creek (Hayward). Residents face seasonal flooding, cold river dampness, and frequent sweeps.
- **Vehicle Dwellers:** Working individuals and elderly seniors sleeping in sedans, station wagons, and converted vans parked along warehouse zones in Newark, Union City, and Fremont.
- **Motel Living:** Single mothers and families living week-to-week along Newark Blvd and Mission Blvd, spending all available funds on exorbitant motel rates just to avoid child protective issues.
- **Senior & Youth Displacement:** A growing demographic of fixed-income seniors displaced by rent hikes, and young adults aging out of foster networks without safety nets.
