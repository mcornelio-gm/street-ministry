There are many ways to serve depending on your comfort level, schedule, and gifts:

### Field: Saturday Morning Route Walker
Walk alongside team leads distributing coffee, hot meals, tarps, and socks.
- **Time commitment:** ~2.5 hours on Saturday mornings
- **Before your first ride-along:** Please read the [Volunteer Orientation & Safety Briefing](/guides/volunteer-guide).

### Kitchen: Friday Meal Prep & Baking
Prepare breakfast burritos, sandwiches, or homemade cookies at home or in the church kitchen for weekend routes.

### Logistics: Supply Sorting & Kit Assembly
Help pack hygiene kits, sort clothing donations, and restock mobile bins on Thursday evenings.
