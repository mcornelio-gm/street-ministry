The Reality on Our Streets Alameda County

Every night, hundreds of our neighbors sleep without security:

  • Creek Beds & Brush: Concealed encampments along Alameda Creek and San Lorenzo Creek.
  • Vehicles & RVs: Families and workers parked in industrial strips and park-and-rides.
  • Weekly Motels: Parents paying high weekly rates to avoid sleeping outdoors with their children.
