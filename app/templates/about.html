About Our Outreach

Loving Our Neighbors Across the East Bay

Resonate Church Street Ministry is an intentional, relational outreach serving our unhoused neighbors across Fremont, Newark, Union City, and Hayward.

Who We Are

We are not a traditional sit-in soup kitchen. We are a mobile, street-level ministry that steps outside church walls each week to meet our vulnerable neighbors right where they are surviving: in tents hidden in creek brush, parked along dark industrial cul-de-sacs, or crammed into budget motels.

Founded by members of Resonate Church, our calling stems from Jesus' clear command in Matthew 25 to feed the hungry, clothe the naked, visit the sick, and welcome the stranger. We believe every person on the street carries the sacred image of God, worthy of friendship, respect, and unconditional love.

Radical Hospitality

Hot coffee, homemade meals, and genuine presence without judgment or agenda.

Relational Consistency

Showing up week in and week out. Trust takes time, and we are committed for the long haul.

Resource Bridging

Connecting friends with Alameda County 2-1-1, winter shelters, and medical navigators.

The Street Reality in Alameda County

Homelessness in the Tri-City and Hayward region is multifaceted:

Creek Encampments

Tucked into the riparian corridors of Alameda Creek (Fremont) and San Lorenzo Creek (Hayward). Residents face seasonal flooding, cold river dampness, and frequent sweeps.

Vehicle Dwellers

Working individuals and elderly seniors sleeping in sedans, station wagons, and converted vans parked along warehouse zones in Newark, Union City, and Fremont.

Motel Living

Single mothers and families living week-to-week along Newark Blvd and Mission Blvd, spending all available funds on exorbitant motel rates just to avoid child protective issues.

Senior & Youth Displacement

A growing demographic of fixed-income seniors displaced by rent hikes, and young adults aging out of foster networks without safety nets.

Donation Wish List

Critical Needs Checklist

We take practical, high-quality supplies directly to the street every week. Currently accepted items:

Winter Warmth

  • Heavy vinyl waterproof tarps (8x10, 10x12)
  • Sub-zero rated sleeping bags & fleece blankets
  • Brand-new wool or thermal crew socks
  • Winter beanies, insulated gloves, hand warmers
  • Rain ponchos with hoods

Hygiene & Essentials

  • Unscented wet wipes (travel packs)
  • Toothbrush & travel toothpaste sets
  • Bar soap & travel shampoo bottles
  • Feminine hygiene products (pads/tampons)
  • First aid kits (bandages, antiseptic ointment)
